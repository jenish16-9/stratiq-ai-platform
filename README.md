# StratIQ

### AI-Powered Executive Decision & Action Platform

StratIQ helps business leaders understand employee turnover, customer churn, and
company performance through evidence-backed AI analysis and governed business
actions.

> StratIQ is an active prototype built with synthetic data for demonstration and
> development purposes.

## Why StratIQ?

Business intelligence is often scattered across spreadsheets, surveys, customer
notes, and dashboards. StratIQ brings these signals together and transforms them
into understandable insights, recommendations, and trackable actions.

## Core Capabilities

### People Intelligence

- Employee turnover-risk analysis
- Engagement and compensation insights
- Employee-level risk profiles
- Evidence-backed explanations

### Customer Retention Intelligence

- Customer churn-risk analysis
- Customer health monitoring
- Revenue and ARR-at-risk insights
- Customer-success note analysis

### Executive Analytics

- KPI dashboards
- Risk summaries
- Morning executive briefs
- Cross-functional alerts

### Governed AI Actions

- Streamed AI analysis
- Inline review forms
- Approval-gated actions
- Internal task creation
- Email notifications
- PDF report generation
- Calendar invitation generation
- Execution history and audit logs
